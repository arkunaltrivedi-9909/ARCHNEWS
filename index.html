About ARCHNEWS

Architecture moves fast.
Staying informed shouldn't be hard.

Architects are problem solvers. But keeping up with 8 different publications, hundreds of weekly stories, and constant industry shifts — that's a problem nobody needed.

ARCHNEWS was built to solve that. One live feed. Eight leading publications. Every story updated automatically, all day. You see what matters — without opening eight tabs.

Every story links directly to its original source. No paywalls introduced. No content stolen. Just the fastest way to know what's happening in architecture right now.

ARCHNEWS Weekly — the Monday morning newsletter — distills the week's five most important stories into one curated briefing. Written by a licensed architect. Not an algorithm.
